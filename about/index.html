Field note no. 01 · What is Elsewhere?

THE ORDINARY
HAS EXITS.

Elsewhere is a free random adventure generator for people who want their day to feel a little less automatic.

Our working theory

Boredom is
a compass.Follow it somewhere small.

01 · The idea

Not a travel planner.
A nudge.

Elsewhere does not ask you to book a flight, spend money, optimize your life, or become a different person. It gives you a small prompt — a detour, a rule, an odd little mission — and lets the familiar world become interesting again.

Some detours take an hour. Some take a minute. All of them are designed to interrupt the loop of doing the same thing in the same way.

02 · How it works

A very small
departure.

  1. 01

    Choose a prompt

    Use the Generator when you want an instant suggestion, or browse the Detour Library for a particular kind of day.

  2. 02

    Follow it loosely

    A detour is an invitation, not an exam. Adapt it to your neighborhood, energy, time, and comfort.

  3. 03

    Keep the proof

    Complete journeys to collect stamps in your Elsewhere Passport — small records of the days you chose differently.

03 · For whom

For curious people
with a day to notice.

Elsewhere is for the person who has a free half hour but no plan; the friend who wants to do something besides scroll; the local who has stopped seeing their own street; and anyone who wants a gentle reason to step outside, look up, or turn left.

You do not need to be brave, athletic, wealthy, or far from home. Start where you are.

04 · The rules

The kind of escape
we believe in.

01

Small enough to start

The best first step is usually the one that does not require a new version of you.

02

Made for real life

Detours work around weather, schedules, budgets, energy, and the fact that ordinary days are already full.

03

Curiosity over productivity

You do not have to post it, monetize it, or turn it into a lesson. It can simply be a good way to spend ten minutes.
